Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

In short: Snore Detect doesn't collect any personal data. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your recordings and results stay on your iPhone unless you choose to share them yourself.

This policy explains how the Snore Detect app for iPhone and iPad ("Snore Detect", "the app") handles your information. Snore Detect is made by an independent developer ("I", "me").

What the app does with your microphone

Snore Detect uses the microphone only while you are recording a night, after you tap Start Recording and allow microphone access. The sound is analysed on your device by Apple's built-in sound classifier to detect snoring. It is never sent to me or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

To show your reports and history, the app stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Night recordings: audio of each night, if Save Audio is turned on (it is on by default, and you can turn it off in Settings).
  • Night results: start and end times, snoring episodes, snore counts and loudness readings.
  • Settings: your sensitivity and Save Audio choices.

Audio recordings are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. Night results and settings may be included in your device backup, depending on your iOS backup settings. Apple handles those backups, and I can't access them.

What I collect

Nothing. Snore Detect has no servers and doesn't connect to the internet. It contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking. I don't receive, sell or share any information about you or how you use the app.

Sharing

Audio leaves your device only if you share it yourself, for example by tapping Share on part of a night's recording and choosing an app or contact in the iOS share sheet. Whatever you share is then handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a night, or just its audio, from the night's report.
  • Delete all saved audio in Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it has stored on your device.

Children

Snore Detect isn't directed at children, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. Because the app collects no data, any change will be about how the app works on your device.
